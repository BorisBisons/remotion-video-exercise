# Character & Style Bible

Every panel is generated against one anchor sheet so skin tones, outfits,
jewellery, lighting and background stay identical across the whole video.
All three characters are original designs written from the script — no
existing artist's characters are referenced or reproduced.

## Locked style block
Ink-and-colour fashion illustration, comic / graphic-novel finish. Fine crisp
black ink outlines, dense hatching and cross-hatching for every shadow and for
hair texture. Flat rich colour fills inside the ink lines. Rich warm brown skin
tones with amber-gold highlights, bold patterned kitenge outfits, gold
jewellery. Plain cream off-white background, empty. Realistic body proportions,
confident fashion-model posing, detailed curly hair. Single warm light from the
upper left in every panel. No text, no logos, no watermark, no signature.

## MUHIRE — 24, bride's side, "the King of Leaves"
Tall, slim, athletic. Deep warm brown skin. High-top curly fade with a sharp
razor line-up, thin moustache, small gold hoop in the left ear. Emerald-green
and gold geometric-print kitenge shirt open at the chest, black slim trousers,
thick gold chain, gold signet ring on the right little finger, white sneakers.
Cocky, chin lifted, talks with his hands.

## GASUKA — 32, groom's side, undercover Inspector
Broader, heavier set. Medium-deep brown skin. Short dense curly hair,
close-trimmed beard, small scar above the right eyebrow. Deep burgundy and
black patterned kitenge blazer over a plain cream shirt, charcoal trousers,
polished brown leather formal lace-up shoes (the tell), plain gold wristwatch.
Watchful, still, unsmiling.

## PAPA (Afande) — 58, Muhire's father, Gasuka's superior
Heavy and broad. Deep brown skin, short grey-flecked curly hair, grey
moustache, gold-rimmed glasses. Navy-blue and gold patterned agbada-cut jacket,
heavy gold ring, black leather shoes. Calm, immovable authority.

## Props
Slatted wooden bench with visible grain, a small twist of brown paper, silver
handcuffs, a police ID badge in a brown leather wallet, a folded banknote.

## Motion note for animation
Characters hold still. Animate only blinks, hair drift, fabric sway and small
hand gestures.
